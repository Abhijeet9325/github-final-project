# Simple Interest Calculator

## 📌 About the Project

The Simple Interest Calculator is a web-based application that calculates simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

It helps users calculate simple interest quickly and easily.

## ✨ Features

- Calculate simple interest.
- Enter the principal amount.
- Enter the annual interest rate.
- Enter the time period in years.
- Display the calculated simple interest.
- Calculate the total amount, including interest.

## 🧮 Formula

Simple Interest is calculated using the following formula:

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where:
- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate (R):** The annual interest rate in percentage.
- **Time (T):** The duration in years.

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the Simple Interest Calculator.
2. Enter the principal amount.
3. Enter the annual interest rate.
4. Enter the time period in years.
5. Click the Calculate button.
6. View the calculated simple interest and total amount.
